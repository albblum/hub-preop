## Section 1 — Legal Basis

**Art. 1** This Act is issued by the Secretary-General of the International Data Reserve (IDR) in exercise of the powers conferred by the Foundational Norm — Pre-Operational Stage, `idr:c:preop-regime:s0:art.en:§4`, during the foundational transitional period.

**Art. 2** The appointment of Provisional Members is authorized for the duration of the pre-operational stage, as defined in `idr:c:preop-regime:s0:art.en:§5`, for the purpose of enabling contribution and deliberation within the DocHUB in the absence of formally constituted committees.

**Art. 3** Each Provisional Member exercises deliberative authority exclusively within the scope and limits established by `idr:c:preop-regime`. No Provisional Member may act beyond the normative boundaries of the pre-operational stage.

> **§2** Participate in deliberation processes registered in the DocHUB ledger.

> **§3** Access all documents of DocumentType `constitutional`, `operational` and `institutional` with status `active` or `foundational-provisional`.

> **§4** The role `provisional_member` does not confer administrative authority over the DocHUB system nor over any instrument outside the deliberative scope defined in `idr:c:preop-regime`.

---

## Section 3 — Validity

**Art. 6** The appointments made by this Act take effect on the date of formal registration in the DocHUB.

**Art. 7** The appointments cease upon whichever of the following conditions occurs first:

> **§1** Formal termination of `idr:c:preop-regime` by act of the Secretary-General, on or before 2026-12-31.

> **§2** Formal constitution of the first IDR committees with active memberships.

**Art. 8** Upon cessation, the role `provisional_member` is automatically suspended for all members appointed by this Act. Suspension does not affect the historical record of contributions and deliberations registered in the DocHUB ledger during the pre-operational stage.
